The six-state permutation algebra

Split a canonical root into distinct CV syllables A B C. First optionally swap A and B, then rotate left zero, once, or twice. The reverse process identifies root and state without a vocabulary of 600 memorized words.

Consonant rail / 4 bits

A fixed spine with binary fins encodes one consonant; the dot on the right encodes one of five vowels.

Phoneme and particle register

Roots use /k l m n ɾ s t v/ with /a e i o u/. Short particles additionally use /d j p/. Each CV rail cell is unambiguous. The second syllable of lexical words bears stress.

Known boundaries of version AZ1

  • Exactly 100 dictionary roots; unknown bags are not assigned meanings.
  • Exactly six forms per root; arbitrary chains normalize to S/L states.
  • Simple declaratives, imperatives, one-level possession, non-nested conditionals, and one-level clause coordination.
  • Full English, cultural pragmatics, arbitrary compound interleaving, and speech synthesis are not supported.
  • Barcode presentation is actual Code 128-B. The app itself validates text from a hardware/software decoder rather than directly scanning camera pixels.
  • The browser is a self-contained HTML artifact: no accounts, network requests, or external JavaScript dependencies.
