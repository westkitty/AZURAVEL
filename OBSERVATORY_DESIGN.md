# The Unwitnessed Index — Experience Contract

## Primary proposition

What appears at first to be an enormous, beautiful field of luminous ribbons is not astronomy, prophecy, or a catalog of dead worlds. The instrument is observing **reversible meaning**: 100 exact three-syllable root identities that each admit six states. The vastness is a true, navigable structure. Investigation turns spectacle into information and information into a question about causal responsibility.

## The viewer's five interpretive turns

1. **Threshold — Misrecognition.** Almost no controls: one decision to cross. The inherited azure ribbon is inviting, but the title challenges the initial visual category. The instrumental status insists it is a local reconstruction, not an actual transmission.
2. **Index — Scale without fabrication.** The animated/canvas constellation consists of actual lexicon objects, 100 roots and 600 six-state marks. Ten sectors of ten entries come from `AZURAVEL_DATA.json`, not generated lore. A small entrance appears to unfold into a large complete mechanism.
3. **Root — A structure that stays itself.** Selecting a sector and a root collapses the field to a specific word identity. The six state labels and corresponding words are computed from canonical JSON; a word is both a visually traversable place and a functional linguistic object.
4. **Counter-event — The reclassification.** `sa EVT` and `CTR` are shown concurrently. The first means no event; the second marks a counter-event or reversal. The viewer is invited to consider the consequence of this difference, not handed a newly invented history. The absence of evidence and the evidence of deliberate removal become two distinct claims.
5. **Witness files — An interpretive afterimage.** Five short, progressively revealed textual case files imagine possible records that a culture using such grammar might preserve. Their linked roots and examples are real, but the stories remain **speculative, not canonical**. The last file introduces second-person deixis and asks whether a language can reserve a role for an addressee before anyone arrives.

## Reality and invention

- **Authoritative:** root IDs, domains, source words, six states, the `sa` negator, the role of `CTR`, the AZ1 morphology, specimen/example sentences, serializer and barcode rules.
- **Interpretive:** witness files, implied archive, danger, any suggestion of a lost population, return, sound suppression or destroyed promise. These may illustrate linguistic affordances but are not accepted historical facts about AZURAVEL or Starsilk.
- **Never claim:** actual remote signal, external access, tracking of a user, physically scannable atmospheric ribbons, memories of real people, a canonical catastrophe, or a relationship to Starsilk canon without new authoritative sources.

## The camera is the interface

Navigation is semantic zoom rather than a sitemap. The route advances `threshold → sectors → roots → forms → differential`, with witness records accessible by a small navigation rail. The same root and form drive semantic content, labels, movement and diagram geometry. The content area takes priority; the interface never occupies more than 40% of a viewport in the measured visual states. Screenshots and the deterministic coverage-probe log are part of QA, not claims of all-device support.

The original user-supplied 6.04-second ribbon video is used as atmospheric light. It is not emitted as a new form of barcode. The field also renders 600 lightweight marks in a single Canvas 2D element; this is an information diagram, not a particle effect. No model is loaded and no WebGL or external component is required. The existing 1.1 lab remains accessible at one explicit link, intact.

## Comfort, accessibility, fallback

The ambient sound is opt-in and defaults off. Reduced motion suppresses video and persistent movement while preserving a static poster and complete interactions. The page avoids flash, automatic camera lurches and unbounded particles. Route changes can be reached by keyboard; readers have semantic text, labels, focus states, an announcer, and a scrollable transcript. Deep links recover the requested root/state. With storage disabled, exploration remains usable. With video failure, the static poster remains. On GitHub Pages or locally beside the original `assets/` folder, both video and poster use relative paths.

## Non-goals

No invented AZ2, new planets, monsters, disaster timeline, external database, synthetic telemetry, fake translation, simulated live signal, speculative codex canonization, decorative number inflation, third-party effects, or fictionalized scanner claims.

## Acceptance evidence

- 100 roots / 600 exact forms; no changes to protected AZ1 source hashes.
- Keyboard and pointer can reach the same meaningful state changes; behavior shown in headless Chromium.
- Every hypothetical reconstruction is labeled `SPECULATIVE / NOT CANON`.
- Combined pre-existing and observatory tests pass; offline deterministic build reproduces `index.html`.
- The `coverage_probe.py` checks seven phases at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, with observed chrome-occupancy below 0.4 and no horizontal overflow.
- Independent public-URL smoke, screen readers, touch hardware, long-session performance, subjective awe and target-device video playback remain separate unverified obligations.
