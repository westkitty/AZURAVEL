## AWE candidate gate (not a feature quota)

- **A: Make the archival map much larger.** Rejected. A large map implies unprovided locations, timestamps, and cause; violates canon and source integrity.
- **B: Add denser particles, sound and 3D.** Rejected. These would compete with the information, add runtime burden and risk breaking reduced-motion comfort.
- **C: The Unclosed Ledger.** Selected. The interactive change alters the visitor's **interpretation** using source-grounded outcomes, cost, and an intimate final object; no new history is needed.

External mechanism comparisons inspected: Forensic Architecture's interactive evidence platforms (explorable evidentiary relations); Neil Halloran's *The Fallen of World War II* (meaningful visualization that pauses to allow investigation); SBS's *The Boat* (layered archive, text and sound used with purpose). Transfer is of the *interaction principle*, not their data, visuals, audio, code, or historical claims. This release depends on no external design package.

## Confirmed QQQ correction

- **Failure:** Saved browser state could contain a non-array `visited`, `roles`, or `witnessSeen`; the old initializer iterated them and threw before the site could be used.
- **Proof before:** `evidence/omni/baseline_corrupt_storage.txt` — Chromium reported `object is not iterable`.
- **Fix:** `loadProgress()` now accepts bounded arrays, ignores malformed items and preserves normal valid progress.
- **Proof after:** `evidence/omni/uplift_corrupt_storage.txt`, plus `test_ledger.py:test_malformed_saved_progress_does_not_break_entry`.
